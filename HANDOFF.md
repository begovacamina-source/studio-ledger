## Phase 3: design polish (started 2026-10-20)

1. ~~First polish PR~~ Done (branch `design/phase-3-polish`): Inter Tight for titles and headings and Inter for body text (self-hosted, tokens `--font-heading` and `--font-sans`); a calm chart, blue Received (`--chart-received`) and dark grey Expenses (`--chart-expenses`), with the legend in bar order and swatches in the tooltip; status hues spread apart (Paid green, Pending amber, Overdue red); on phones, a menu button on the left of the top bar opens a dropdown with the four tabs and, below a divider, Settings as text (the gear shows from md up), so no page scrolls sideways at 375 px; and a minimal design pass (borderless cards with a soft shadow on a tinted page, 24 px card padding, `space-y-8` between sections, lighter table dividers, consistent corners). Before and after screenshots are in `docs/screenshots/before/` and `docs/screenshots/after/`.
2. ~~Card lists and pagination~~ Done (branch `feature/ux-8-mobile-card-lists`, stacked on the first polish PR): below md every table is a card list (`src/components/card-list.tsx`, only one layout renders, via `useIsMobile`), and Invoices, Expenses and the import preview show 10 rows per page with shadcn's pagination (`src/components/list-pagination.tsx`, logic in `src/lib/pagination.ts`, page in the URL through `usePageParam`). The Overview's Outstanding list shows the 10 most urgent invoices. The invoice list switches to the card list below 1024 px instead (`useIsBelow(LG_BREAKPOINT)`), because its seven columns don't fit a tablet.
3. ~~Clients page~~ Done (US-2.5, Should): `/clients` (between Invoices and Expenses in the nav) lists each client with total Received and total Outstanding over all time and the number of invoices, highest Received first, as a table or, below md, a card list, 10 per page. A client opens in a side panel at `/clients/<id>` with its totals and invoices (each links to its invoice panel). "Delete client" explains and offers "Show their invoices" while the client has invoices, and deletes after a confirmation once it has none. Logic is in `src/lib/clients.ts`.
4. ~~Undo for marking paid~~ Done (US-2.2, Should): Mark as paid and Mark as not paid each show a toast with Undo for 5 seconds (`UNDO_WINDOW_MS`), and the invoice panel shows the same Undo inside it, because a modal panel keeps keyboard focus away from the toast. Undo restores only the status and paid date (`restorePaidStatus`). One undo at a time lives in `src/state/undo-provider.tsx`; marking goes through `usePaidActions()`. The toaster takes clicks over modals, and clicking a toast doesn't close a panel (`src/components/ui/sheet.tsx`). UX-4 is checked.
5. Next: the full design review on the Vercel preview (UX-10), and a full contrast check (A11Y-3).

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` runs lint, unit tests, the build and the journeys on every pull request and push to `main`, and keeps the Playwright report as an artifact.
